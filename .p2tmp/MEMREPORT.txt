STRONGHOLD-PROTOCOL — WHERE THE 584 MB GOES (read-only investigation)
====================================================================
Nothing was restarted, reloaded, deployed or reconfigured. The live box was only
read (3 bounded samples: /healthz + ps + free -m + systemctl show + 3 cgroup files).
No file under server/** or public/js/** was touched; probes live in
F:\WeChat\Stronghold-Protocol\.p2tmp\mem\* and F:\WeChat\.p2tmp\peekmem.sh.
Local tree measured: 0.1.4. Live build: 0.1.3 (1c7c3b07a6c6) — its /healthz has no
"mem" field, so no live heapUsed exists; live V8 slack numbers below are inferred.

HOW THE PER-UNIT NUMBERS WERE OBTAINED
--------------------------------------
Virtual-scheduler harness (test/match/harness.js, makeMatch + Scheduler virtual=true),
node --expose-gc, snapshot after two synchronous major GCs, then the MARGINAL method:
build+drive batch 1, snapshot, build+drive batch 2 of the same kind, take the slope.
The second batch pays no one-time module/hidden-class/string-table cost, so the slope is
the honest per-match figure. (Batch totals without this correction are 1.5-2x too high;
per-key ablation is below the noise floor and was discarded.) "Server-side" means the
in-process SimClient battle objects were nulled before the snapshot, so what remains is
what the server itself retains for a client-combat match. Config: NORMAL, 2 human seats
unless stated. Every number is +/-15-20%.

RANKED DECOMPOSITION OF THE 584 MB PEAK (and of the 459 MB I sampled)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Match plane, base per live match ....... ~170-215 MB  [per-unit MEASURED, total ESTIMATED]
    854 matches x ~200-250 KB average. Base grows with round depth (99 KB at R1 -> 353 KB
    at R7 -> 425 KB at R13, no battles involved); most of it is per-match PlayerState
    payload, the retained view JSON strings, and per-match GameData memo caches.
 2. V8 committed-but-unused heap ........... ~150-200 MB  [MEASURED locally, ESTIMATED live]
    Live heap is far below RSS: locally heapTotal was 4.6x heapUsed and RSS 6x, and RSS
    stayed ~200 MB after dispose()+GC. This is the term that makes 300 MB of live data
    show up as ~500 MB of RSS, and it is the reason the peak overshoots the live set.
 3. Bot / takeover field battle graphs ..... ~55-90 MB   [per-unit MEASURED, total ESTIMATED]
    471 bot seats x ~115 KB (R1) to ~395 KB (R7) while the field exists; battles exist
    only during COMBAT (~40% duty) and are released at phase end. Each seat taken over
    from an absent human adds a similar graph.
 4. Process baseline ........................ ~79 MB  [MEASURED]
    node runtime + 4.15 MB of data/*.json expanded to +8.6 MB heap / +23.5 MB RSS +
    the loaded module graph (booted, zero rooms).
 5. Sockets/sessions + rooms/replay ......... ~35-55 MB  [sockets MEASURED, replay ESTIMATED]
    1,826 ws connections x 11.5 KB heap (13.8 KB RSS) ~= 22-25 MB; 742 rooms holding
    replay frames/lastSummary after their match ended, ~15-40 KB each, ~= 11-30 MB.
 Sum ~420-540 MB -> the sampled 459 MB and the 584 MB peak are both explained without
 any unknown leak. Order of the top two is firm; 3 vs 4 depends on the COMBAT duty cycle.

RANKED LEVERS (MB saved at the observed peak: 854 matches / 471 bots / 157 paused)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Cap the heap: NODE_OPTIONS=--max-old-space-size=384..448 (one env var, no code).
    Saves ~100-150 MB of term 2 by keeping V8's committed free space from ballooning.
    Cheapest change; determinism-neutral (GC timing is invisible to a seeded, tick-driven
    sim). Cost: more GC CPU on an already 71-73% busy single core.
 2. Bound concurrency: SP_MAX_ROOMS 2500 -> ~900-1000 (env only). 2500 rooms x ~250 KB is
    ~600 MB of match plane alone, more than the 900 MiB cgroup and about half the 1967 MB
    host. Saves up to ~250 MB in the worst case, ~0 in the current normal case.
 3. Expire paused matches / evict their graphs: 40-130 MB at 157 paused (see Q1 below).
    Requires a small code change under server/** (or a shorter SP_SOLO_RECONNECT_MS).
 4. Share the per-match GameData memo caches instead of rebuilding them per Match:
    24-57 KB x 854 ~= 20-49 MB (memo maps over deep-frozen shared data -> hoist keyed by
    (data, modeId)). Code change, determinism-neutral.
 5. Trim retained view caches (_lastPubJson/_lastPriv, 9-51 KB per match): 8-44 MB; also
    the single fastest-growing per-round term. Drop them on pause/end and for dead seats.
 6. Clear f.battle as soon as a field completes (complete() keeps run.battle until the
    phase ends): 100-480 KB per finished-but-retained field. Small in steady state.
 7. Sockets/rooms: 35-55 MB total. Not worth touching (11.5 KB per connection measured).
 NOT levers: Match construction cost (GameData 4.1 KB + pool 9.2 KB = 13.3 KB / 92 us per
 match — the known 12.7 KB / 82 us figure is confirmed; only ~11-14 MB in total, and it is
 the *driven* memo caches up to 57 KB that add up), worker threads (nlwp 11, none used),
 and SP_VERIFY (already off).

ANSWERS TO THE FOUR QUESTIONS
-----------------------------
 Q1. Shrinking SP_SOLO_RECONNECT_MS / capping paused matches that keep graphs.
     IDLE-PAUSE frees almost nothing: _freeze() only cancels timers and keeps f.job,
     f.battle, f.spec, f.timeline (Match.js L1199-1218); measured 349 KB paused vs 359 KB
     active at R1 — i.e. a paused match costs the same as a running one. So the saving is
     the whole footprint of whatever stops being retained: 157 paused x ~250-350 KB
     = ~40-55 MB central (7-9% of the peak), up to ~140 MB if each of the 157 is an
     abandoned co-op match still holding 2 takeover + ~1 bot battle graph at mid-depth,
     and as little as ~25 MB if they are solo runs frozen in early PREP. Evicting only the
     battle graphs (keeping the resume) captures the field layer, ~40 MB of that.
 Q2. Raw match count vs bots. The match count is the dominant term: 854 x ~100-425 KB
     depending on depth ~= 100-280 MB (central ~200 MB). Bots are second: 471 seats x
     ~115-480 KB per graph while it exists, damped by the ~40% COMBAT duty ~= 55-90 MB.
     Ended matches are cheap (Match is disposed; ~13 KB + replay frames), so it is LIVE
     matches that matter, not matches played.
 Q3. Is MemoryMax binding? No — and the configured value is not the one in the task.
     The live unit has MemoryMax=943718400 (900 MiB), not 1258291200. Node's peak RSS was
     584 MB, and memory.current tracks RSS + ~33 MB, so the cgroup would need ~910 MB of
     RSS to breach; memory.peak since the restart is 508 MB, with memory.events oom 0,
     oom_kill 0. The host, however, has 1967 MB total with ~1475 MB used by everything and
     only ~490 MB available, no swap in use. Conclusion: the cgroup cap is not binding and
     the kill at 584 MB looks like the HOST-level OOM killer choosing the largest
     reclaimable process. (Caveat: the surviving counters are post-restart, so a
     pre-restart cgroup breach cannot be formally excluded — but 584 MB < 900 MiB makes it
     unlikely.)
 Q4. Cheapest change that most reduces OOM risk at peak concurrency: cap the process
     (--max-old-space-size=384..448) AND cap rooms (SP_MAX_ROOMS ~900-1000), then expire
     paused matches. Both are env-only, and both are determinism-safe: the simulator is
     seeded and tick-driven and reads no wall clock, and a dropped battle graph is rebuilt
     from the same frozen BattleSpec (the code already re-simulates for bot seats, for
     takeovers, and for SP_VERIFY), so results are bit-identical. Nothing here touches
     step order, seeds or the tick loop.

WHAT COULD NOT BE MEASURED
--------------------------
 - Live heapUsed/heapTotal: the deployed 0.1.3 /healthz has no mem field, so the V8 slack
   term (2) is inferred from local ratios, not read from the box.
 - The real live mix: no per-match round depth, phase, or count of abandoned seats is
   exposed (fields 0 / fieldsIdle 0 while CPU was 71-73%, because bot jobs finish in
   milliseconds and their battles stay retained after complete()). The match-plane total is
   therefore an estimate built from measured per-depth unit costs.
 - No heap snapshot or profiler on the live box (forbidden) and none locally (too heavy):
   attribution is by group ablation with a large signal, not by a dominator tree; ~15-30%
   of the per-match cost stays unattributed (PlayerState objects themselves, bot recall
   caches, per-round bookkeeping).
 - Room replay / lastSummary byte size is read from code (lobby.js buildReplay/disposeRoom),
   not measured.
 - Whether the 157 paused matches are solo or abandoned co-op (which sets the width of Q1)
   is not derivable from /healthz.
 - The cgroup counters are post-restart, so the exact pre-kill memory.current is unknown.
